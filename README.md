# scripture-context

Version 11 is an interactive timeline connecting events, people, and teachings across the Bible and Book of Mormon.

Choose an era, then use the category buttons to show or hide tracks and events. Drag or tap the timeline to select a year, or use **Jump to an event** to open its study card. **Fit All** restores the full era at the current year. The range label shows elapsed years between the endpoints, with one year between 1 BC and AD 1.

The collapsible **Scripture Context** panel sits beneath the timeline. Its cards link to passages and distinguish broad study windows from events with unknown calendar dates. In the Christ era, separate event lanes keep the New Testament and Book of Mormon accounts visible together. The background legend identifies the regional or narrative setting for each view.

Open `index.html` in a browser to preview the site. GitHub Pages serves `index.html` and `study-data.js` together from the repository root. There is no build step.
