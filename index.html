Dates in other eras may be illustrative traditional chronologies. Solid event markers indicate historically anchored dates; dashed markers and ranges indicate approximate or traditional placement. Later scripture references are not dated to the historical events they describe. Dates are approximate unless otherwise specified. The bars show reigns, narrative periods, or periods of recorded activity—not necessarily lifespans. In the Jerusalem era, Lehi's departure is traditionally dated around 600 BC, but 1 Nephi 1 places its opening in Zedekiah's first year (historically 597 BC). The two dating conventions are not fully reconciled. Scripture links open ChurchofJesusChrist.org. The dataset is selective: missing event markers do not imply a gap in scripture or history. Built for study, not as a claim of precise dating for every passage.
